## Step-by-Step Bathroom Work & Cost Itemization

### 1. Demolition, Cleaning & Waste Disposal (€250 - €450)
Removing old tiles, mosaic floor, bathtub, sink, and old piping. Includes bagging and authorized waste disposal.

### 2. Plumbing Replacement (€400 - €750)
Replacing old pipes with polypropylene (PPR) pipes, installing floor drains, and positioning outlets for wall-hung toilet frames, faucets, and water heaters.

### 3. Waterproofing & Sloped Screed (€300 - €550)
Applying 2 coats of flexible two-component waterproofing membrane with corner sealing tape, followed by sloped screed toward floor drains.

### 4. Tiling & Grouting (€800 - €1,600)
Laying ceramic or porcelain tiles. Large-format tiles (60x120 cm or 120x280 cm) require specialized handling and double-sided adhesive spreading.

### 5. Moisture-Proof Ceiling & Ventilation (€200 - €380)
Installing green moisture-resistant drywall with recessed spotlights and a check-valve exhaust fan.

### 6. Sanitary Fixtures & Shower Screen (€350 - €700)
Setting up wall-hung toilet, faucets, shower system, vanity sink, LED mirror, and glass shower enclosure.

## Pro Tips to Save Money Without Sacrificing Quality

1. **Never skip waterproofing tape:** Two-component waterproofing costs under €100 for materials, protecting against thousands in potential leak damages.
2. **Choose a linear drain with dry trap:** Dry traps prevent sewer odor backflow even if the bathroom isn't used for days.
3. **Use epoxy grout for floors:** Unlike cementitious grout, epoxy grout is non-porous, mold-resistant, and maintains its color indefinitely.
