## 4 Hidden Costs in Sofia Renovations (That Builders Forget)

1. **Plastering for 90° Right Angles:**  
   New construction walls in Sofia frequently have 1 to 3 cm deviations. Built-in wardrobes and kitchens require exact 90° right angles, requiring extra plastering labor.
2. **Plumbing & Electrical Repositioning:**  
   Developer-installed electrical outlets and plumbing connections rarely match custom layouts. Moving them mid-project costs 3x more if not pre-planned.
3. **Logistics, Parking & Carrying Fees:**  
   Sofia parking restrictions and carrying materials without freight elevators add up to 10% in transport fees.
4. **Contingency Reserve (10-15%):**  
   Always maintain a buffer for hidden structural defects or extra reinforcement.
