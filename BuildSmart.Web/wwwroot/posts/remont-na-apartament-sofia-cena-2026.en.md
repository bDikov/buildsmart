## Where Do 90% of Homeowners Get Trapped?

The single biggest mistake is comparing quotes at face value. When one builder quotes €15,000 and another quotes €25,000, 9 times out of 10, the cheaper quote **simply omitted essential work** (waterproofing, debris disposal, primers, quality wiring, and floor leveling).

The result? Mid-project "unexpected additions" that make the cheap quote far more expensive than the honest high quote.
