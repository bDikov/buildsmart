## 2. Breaking Down the 3 Biggest Pitfalls of Traditional Renovations

### Risk #1: The Upfront Cash Trap & Abandoned Jobs
The most frequent complaint among Sofia homeowners is handing over several thousand Euros for "materials and slot reservation" before work even begins. Once paid, dishonest crews juggle multiple jobs simultaneously, show up for 2 hours a day, or vanish altogether.
- **The BuildSmart Standard:** We operate strictly with **0% advance deposit**. Funds remain protected in a digital escrow and are only transferred to the crew after you have inspected and signed off on each milestone.

### Risk #2: The Hidden Surcharge Spiral
A lack of detailed itemization allows contractors to tack on surprise fees for basic tasks: carrying bags upstairs, dumping rubble at licensed landfills, window reveals, and 45-degree tile mitering.
- **The BuildSmart Standard:** Every task is calculated upfront using standard building norms across 200+ line items. The contractual price is locked.

### Risk #3: Substandard Tools & Zero Accountability
Unregulated builders often lack laser levels, dustless sanding machinery, or professional wet tile saws. The result: uneven corners, cracked plaster, and loose tiles within months.
- **The BuildSmart Standard:** Every crew in our network is vetted and required to follow strict building codes with professional-grade machinery.
