# Real Case Study: Full Renovation Budget for a 3-Room Apartment (100 sq.m.) in Sofia

To give you the most accurate real-world overview, here is an anonymized cost calculation from a completed project in Sofia (**100 sq.m., 2 bedrooms, living room with kitchen, and 2 full bathrooms**).

---

## Work Scope & Equipment Summary

- **Installations & HVAC:** Multi-split climate system (3 indoor units + 1 outdoor unit with anti-freeze tray), full plumbing replacement for 2 bathrooms + kitchen, electrical wiring for LED coves and security cameras.
- **Construction Work:** Suspended ceilings with LED profiles, 2 coats of plastering, painting, imported tiles, 2 shower cabins, natural stone thresholds, 5 solid MDF interior doors with high acoustic insulation.
- **Furnishings & Appliances:** Custom oak kitchen with synthetic stone countertop (€8,200), hallway wardrobe (€2,050), dining table & chairs, bedroom set, sofa, living room wall unit, and full appliance suite (oven, hob, extractor, fridge, dishwasher, washing machine, dryer).
