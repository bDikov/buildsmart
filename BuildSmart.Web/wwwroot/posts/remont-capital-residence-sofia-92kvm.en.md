# Real Case Study: Full Turnkey Renovation of a 3-Room Flat (92 sq.m) in Capital Residence Sofia

Here is a comprehensive cost breakdown and milestone overview of a real-world renovation and full furnishing project for a brand-new **92 sq.m** 3-room apartment in the modern **Capital Residence Sofia** complex (Central Railway Station / Center district).

The flat was delivered as a bare shell (rough plaster and screed) by the developer and completely finished turnkey with 2 fully equipped designer bathrooms, recessed plumbing, suspended ceilings with hidden LED coves, and custom furniture.

---

## 1. Scope of Work

### Electrical & Plumbing Systems
- **Full Electrical Overhaul:** New dedicated circuits for heavy kitchen appliances, a modern distribution board with residual current devices (RCD), data cabling, and smart LED wiring.
- **Recessed Plumbing:** Wall and floor channelling for polypropylene (PPR) water pipes and PVC drainage lines across both bathrooms and the open kitchen.
- **Fixtures & Drainage:** Installed concealed shower systems, laundry lines, and slim stainless linear floor drains with anti-odour dry valves, followed by structural mortar patching.

### Drywall, Plastering & Designer Finishes
- **Suspended Ceilings & Lighting Coves:** Moisture-resistant and standard drywall ceilings in the kitchen and both bathrooms with integrated indirect LED lighting coves.
- **Plaster Skimming & Corner Alignment:** Full wall reinforcement with fiberglass mesh and galvanized corner beads for sharp 90-degree angles, followed by finishing plaster and dustless machine sanding.
- **Decorative Plaster:** Premium accent wall finish in the open-plan living room.
- **Painting:** Deep-penetrating primer and 2 airless-sprayed coats of premium washable latex paint.

### 2 Turnkey Bathrooms
- Two-coat elastic waterproofing membrane with sealing tapes on all wall-floor junctions.
- Large-format porcelain tiling with 45-degree mitered external corners.
- Concealed toilet cisterns (Grohe), wall-hung rimless toilets, bidet sprayers, thermostatic showers, and tempered glass screens.

### Flooring & Doors
- Porcelain tiles in hallways and sanitary areas.
- High-grade moisture-resistant laminate flooring in bedrooms and living area.
- 4 solid painted MDF interior doors with enhanced soundproofing.
- Designer switches, sockets, and architectural lighting fixtures.

## 3. Project Schedule & Milestones

- **Construction Work (Turnkey Finish):** **3 months** from start date to a completely finished, spotlessly cleaned shell ready for furniture installation.
- **Custom Furniture Production & Fitting:** **3.5 months**, running concurrently with final finishing stages to minimize total vacancy time.

---

## 4. How the Homeowner was Protected

1. **0% Advance Deposit:** The owner never paid any cash upfront. Payments were made milestone-by-milestone only after each stage was formally inspected and accepted.
2. **Fixed-Price Contract:** The €38,000 construction scope was locked via an itemized Bill of Quantities on Day 1 with zero hidden markups.
3. **Written Guarantee:** The property received a multi-year warranty certificate covering all MEP installations and tile work.
