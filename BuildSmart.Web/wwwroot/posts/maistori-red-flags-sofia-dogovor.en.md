# 5 Contractor Red Flags: How to Avoid Renovation Scams in Sofia

Choosing the right builder or renovation company in Sofia can save you thousands of Euros or drag you into a multi-month nightmare. Unreliable contractors often rely on the same tricks to hook clients with unrealistically low upfront quotes.

Here are 5 major red flags to watch for before signing any renovation deal in Bulgaria.

---

## 1. Demanding 50%+ Upfront Deposit

If a builder demands half the budget upfront for "materials" or "reserving dates", proceed with extreme caution. This is the single most common tactic used by dishonest crews before abandoning projects halfway through.

> **How BuildSmart Works:** We operate with **0% advance deposit**. Payments are made strictly in stages — after each milestone is completed and approved by you on site.

---

## 2. Lump-Sum Quotes Without Itemization

Quotes that state: *"Full bathroom renovation – €3,500 total"* are loaded with hidden traps. Without an itemized scope (sq.m. plastering, linear meters of piping, electrical outlets), contractors can easily charge extra for "unexpected labor".

---

## 3. No Written Contract or Delay Penalty Clauses

Without a signed contract containing daily delay penalties, a 2-month renovation can stretch past 6 months. A valid contract must include:
- Fixed completion deadline.
- Fixed final price with no unilateral fee hikes.
- Daily financial penalty for unexcused delays.

---

## 4. Refusing Written Warranties

Professional contractors guarantee their workmanship in writing. If a contractor offers only a "verbal promise", any plumbing leaks or drywall cracks occurring 6 months later will be fixed at your own expense.

---

## 5. Omitted Material Carrying & Waste Disposal Fees

Property owners are often tempted by cheap labor quotes, only to learn later that carrying heavy cement bags upstairs or hauling debris to an authorized landfill is billed under "unexpected extra tariffs".
