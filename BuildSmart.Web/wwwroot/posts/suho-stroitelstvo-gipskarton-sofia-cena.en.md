## System Types & Pricing Breakdown

### 1. Wall Lining (€18 - €28 / sq.m.)
Installing metal framing (CD/UD profiles) and plasterboard sheets over uneven brick or concrete walls. Ideal for concealing wiring and leveling surfaces.

### 2. Single-Level Suspended Ceiling (€22 - €35 / sq.m.)
Single-tier metal grid with single or double plasterboard. Conceals electrical lines and supports recessed spotlights.

### 3. Multi-Level Decorative Ceilings with LED Coves (€35 - €60 / sq.m.)
Custom architectural ceiling boxes featuring hidden LED cove lighting and recessed curtain tracks.

### 4. Acoustic Mineral Wool Insulation (€8 - €15 / sq.m. additional)
Adding dense acoustic mineral wool (50 kg/m³) inside framing cavities to block noise from neighbors and improve thermal retention.
